copyright its contributors and is normally distributed under GPL-2.0-or-later;
some builds may be configured under LGPL-2.1-or-later. Each managed runtime
release includes the upstream packaging notices, exact mpv source archive,
the pinned Windows builder source, checksum manifest, and build recipes.
